Spinal Energetics

Spinal Energetics is a gentle healing practice that sees the spine as the main channel for our life force energy. Through light touch, it activates your body's natural healing response, releasing stored tension and trauma so transformation can unfold at your own pace.

How Spinal Energetics Works

1. Gentle Touch/Intention

Light contact points along your spine and nervous system activate your body's natural healing response.

2. Energy Release

Your body begins to release stored tension, emotions, and trauma through natural movements and responses.

3. Integration

Your nervous system integrates the journey, creating new patterns of wellness and vitality.

Benefits You May Experience

Physical Healing

  • Relief from chronic pain and tension
  • Improved spinal alignment and posture
  • Enhanced nervous system function
  • Increased energy and vitality

Emotional & Mental Wellness

  • Release of stored emotional trauma
  • Reduced anxiety and stress patterns
  • Greater emotional resilience
  • Improved mental clarity and focus

What to Expect

Energy is not bound by time or space, so sessions work just as well ONLINE - a perfect alternative when travel isn't an option, or your own home simply feels easier to relax in.

Preparation

  • Download Zoom - you'll get the link by email about 15 minutes before we start.
  • Set up a comfortable spot to lie down undisturbed, camera positioned so I can see your whole body.
  • Use headphones or external speakers, and wear loose, comfortable clothing.
  • Eat lightly before the session - the experience is often stronger on an empty stomach.

During & After

We begin with a short conversation to create a safe space, then you lie comfortably while I make gentle contact along your spine. Your body may respond with movement, sound, emotion, or deep relaxation - all responses are welcome.

Integration continues afterwards - you may feel shifts for days or weeks. Stay hydrated, rest when needed, and honor whatever arises.

Good to Know

Single sessions can be transformative - but a series deepens the process and integration over time.

Avoid treatment if you have:

  • Severe mental illness (psychosis, schizophrenia, acute bipolar disorder)
  • PTSD
  • Epilepsy (risk of seizures)
  • Serious back problems (acute disc herniation, unstable spine)
  • Osteoporosis with fracture risk
  • Pacemaker or other electronic implants
  • Pregnancy (especially first trimester)
  • Serious heart problems (recent heart attack, unstable blood pressure)
  • Under 12 years old
  • Under the influence of alcohol or drugs
