Breathwork

Conscious Breathwork is an ancient practice that uses specific breathing techniques to facilitate profound healing, emotional release, and spiritual awakening - creating a safe space for whatever needs to move through you.

How Breathwork Works

1. Conscious Breathing

Using specific breathing patterns to access altered states of consciousness and bypass the analytical mind.

2. Emotional Release

Allowing suppressed emotions and limiting beliefs to surface and be released from the body and mind.

3. Integration

Processing and integrating the insights and healing that occurred, creating lasting transformation.

Benefits You May Experience

Physical & Emotional

  • Deep emotional release and healing
  • Reduced stress and anxiety
  • Increased energy and vitality
  • Release of physical tension

Mental & Spiritual

  • Enhanced spiritual connection
  • Greater self-awareness and clarity
  • Access to inner wisdom
  • Expanded consciousness

What to Expect

Preparation

  • Download Zoom - you'll get the link about 30 minutes before we start.
  • Prepare a comfortable, undisturbed place to lie down, and bring water.
  • Use headphones or external speakers, tested beforehand for good sound.
  • Wear loose, comfortable clothing, and avoid a heavy meal beforehand.

During & After

We begin with guided breathing techniques in a safe container - allow yourself to breathe, feel, and release whatever needs to move through you.

Afterwards, take time to integrate - stay hydrated, rest, and be gentle with yourself as any insights or emotions continue to surface.

Good to Know

Breathwork may not be appropriate if you are pregnant or living with cardiovascular disease, epilepsy, asthma or other respiratory conditions, or mental health conditions.
