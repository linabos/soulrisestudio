Source Transmission - Life Force Activation

An intuitive, experience-based practice that awakens your life force and helps you reconnect with who you truly are. Expect anything from deep relaxation to spontaneous movement or emotional release - whatever your body needs, unfolding naturally at its own pace.

How Life Force Activation Works

1. Energy

Through energy transmission, your life force is gently awakened and activated.

2. Natural Response

Your body responds naturally through movement, emotions, sensations, or deep relaxation as energy blocks release.

3. Integration

The journey continues as your system integrates the new energy patterns, creating lasting transformation.

Benefits You May Experience

Energy & Vitality

  • Awakened life force energy
  • Increased vitality and aliveness
  • Enhanced sense of purpose
  • Greater connection to your true self

Emotional & Spiritual

  • Release of old patterns and trauma
  • Expanded awareness and presence
  • Deeper spiritual connection
  • Greater emotional freedom

What to Expect

Energy is not bound by time or space, so sessions work just as well ONLINE - a perfect alternative when travel isn't an option, or your own home simply feels easier to relax in.

Preparation

  • Download Zoom - you'll get the link by email about 15 minutes before we start.
  • Set up a comfortable spot to lie down undisturbed, camera positioned so I can see your whole body.
  • Use headphones or external speakers, and wear loose, comfortable clothing.
  • Eat lightly and skip caffeine that day - the experience is often stronger on an empty stomach.

During & After

We begin with a short conversation to create a safe space, then you lie comfortably while I guide the energy activation. Nothing is required from you - simply relax and allow.

Integration continues afterwards - you may feel shifts for days or weeks. Drink plenty of water, rest when needed, and honor whatever arises.

Not Suitable If You Are

Pregnant (3rd trimester) · under 18 · under the influence of alcohol or drugs · living with epilepsy or a history of psychosis
