Reiki Healing

Reiki is an ancient Japanese healing practice that channels universal life force energy to balance your body, mind, and spirit. Performed at a distance, you simply lie down and relax while the energy flows intuitively to where it's needed - all you need to do is receive.

How Reiki Works

1. Energy Flow

Universal life force energy is channeled to the recipient.

2. Natural Healing

The energy flows to where it's needed most, supporting the body's natural healing processes.

3. Balance & Peace

Deep relaxation and inner peace emerge as energy blockages are gently released.

Benefits You May Experience

Physical & Emotional

  • Deep relaxation and stress relief
  • Balanced energy and emotional healing
  • Enhanced sense of peace and well-being
  • Reduced anxiety and tension

Spiritual & Mental

  • Spiritual connection and clarity
  • Enhanced intuition and awareness
  • Mental clarity and focus
  • Release of energetic blockages

What to Expect

Preparation

  • Wear comfortable, loose clothing.
  • Eat lightly before the session.
  • Drink plenty of water before and after.
  • Set an intention for healing.

During & After

You choose a comfortable place to lie down and relax, while I connect to the universal energy and let it flow intuitively to where it's needed. You may notice warmth, tingling, deep relaxation, or reduced discomfort.

We close with a short conversation about what you experienced. Take time to rest, drink water, and let the healing continue naturally.
